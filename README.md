Rotageek provides workforce management and scheduling solutions, offering digital rota planning, staff forecasting, labour modelling, auto‑scheduling, AI insights and HR integration. Their platform helps businesses optimise staff rotas, predict demand spikes, and improve employee engagement through a suite of cloud‑based tools.

**Areas:** Company · Workforce Management · Scheduling · AI-Insights · HR Integration

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Rotageek: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
